Privacy policy

ESM pipeline · effective 19 October 2026

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and allow access to Google Drive, ESM pipeline uses that access for one purpose only: it copies a specific public file (Oracle's Elixir League of Legends match data) into your Google Drive, downloads that copy to the computer that runs the tool, and then deletes the copy.

ESM pipeline does not read, list, change or delete any other file in your Google Drive, and it does not access your email, contacts or profile data.

What is stored

The sign-in token that Google issues is stored only on the computer that runs the tool. ESM pipeline has no server of its own and does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Sharing

No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. ESM pipeline's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can remove ESM pipeline's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
